its rounding ORDER, which is what makes the MEAS-16 anchor reproduce to six
decimal places — and give the arena a canonical store for reading and writing
`experiments/baselines/` records.

paths (`experiments/run_public.py` and `arena/`) must agree on
`0.92 / 0.524466 / 3.425 / 0.7575 / 0.76884`. D-06 keeps `run_public.py` frozen
precisely so that agreement is evidence rather than tautology, and D-08 forbids
importing `metric_summary`, so the duplication here is load-bearing, not debt.

the shared arena test fixtures, and the anchor test module (MEAS-03, MEAS-16).
