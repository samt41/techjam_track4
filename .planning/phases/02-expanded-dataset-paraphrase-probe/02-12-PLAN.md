<objective>
Author, gate, freeze and commit the two expanded corpora — `expanded_dev.v1` (2,000 sessions) and
`expanded_confirm.v1` (800) — with Haiku 4.5 (D-49), enforcing D-27's split discipline by
construction rather than by intent.

Purpose: MEAS-10 asks for an expanded evaluation set beyond the original 200 where every session
takes the evaluator's authored-card branch, verified programmatically. D-25 sizes `expanded_dev` at
2,000 so its MDD (~0.020) sits exactly inside the project's stated 0.02-0.03 decision band; D-26
sizes `expanded_confirm` at 800 because the private set is 800, which makes its power argument
self-evident. D-27 makes the split real: a different seed label, a different prompt revision and a
disjoint target sample, with `expanded_confirm` unread until Phase 5. D-49 additionally means the
corpus Phases 3-4 tune on and the corpus carrying the headline finding no longer share a generator,
so tuning cannot overfit to the finding's generator.

This is also where the asymmetric solvability rule is exercised in the direction that is ALLOWED:
`--solvability-check` runs here and was refused for the probe in plan 02-09 (D-35, L-3).

Output: two committed corpora, two frozen response logs, two registry entries, and the disjointness
and conformance sweeps that prove the split discipline held.
</objective>
