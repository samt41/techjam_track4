<objective>
Build the D-32 anti-circularity mechanism: a document-frequency-gated attribute gist that is the
ONLY thing the authoring model ever sees about a target, plus the D-52 feature-abstraction table
that closes the measured 8% hole in D-32's stated rationale.

Purpose: MEAS-12's core claim is that the target's literal catalog text never appears in the
authoring prompt. Research measured that a DF floor alone does not deliver this for
`Attribute.FEATURE` — 11,522 of 136,232 feature values clear DF≥2 and the high-DF survivors are
verbatim catalog boilerplate (`imported` 13,832; `machine wash` 8,899; `rubber sole` 5,616) — while
`feature` is 50.5% of all control constraints and cannot simply be dropped (L-6). D-52 closes this
with a committed hand-written abstraction table. The claim then becomes a data-flow property that
a test can assert, not a prompt-discipline instruction someone could violate.

Output: `arena/datasets/gist.py`, two committed JSON assets, the MEAS-12 gist tests, and the
`docs/STATUS.md` honesty-tier entries CONVENTIONS.md requires for every new tuned constant.
</objective>
