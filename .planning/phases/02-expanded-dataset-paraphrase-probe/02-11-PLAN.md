<objective>
Author, gate, freeze and commit `data/probe.v1.jsonl` — the artifact this phase's headline finding
rests on — then convert every gate from a fixture-level test into an assertion over the real
committed file.

Purpose: D-29 is explicit that the probe is protected ahead of the expanded corpora, so it is
authored first. Roadmap Success Criteria 2, 3, 4 and 5 all land here: matched pairs on identical
targets, an anti-circular authoring pipeline with a measured per-pair overlap ratio as an acceptance
gate, a second-model cross-check arm with its gap reported rather than assumed absent, and a
checksummed freeze recorded before any Phase 3 or Phase 4 candidate is measured against it.

SC3's "for every probe pair" is the part that is easy to lose. The generator computes an overlap
ratio for every authored constraint and, before plan 02-05's sidecar existed, threw it away after
the gate decision — leaving only `bucket_summary`'s per-bucket means, which cannot answer a
per-pair question. This plan commits `data/divergence.probe.v1.jsonl` (one record per
`(pair_id, arm, slot, position)`, both arms) and then does the thing that makes it evidence rather
than bookkeeping: re-derives every ratio from the committed `data/targets.probe.v1.json` snapshot
and asserts it equals the recorded one.

The authoring step is operator-run: `claude -p` authenticates via the operator's interactive OAuth
session (`--bare` explicitly excludes OAuth), and the run is hours of wall-clock. Everything after
it is autonomous.
</objective>
