<objective>
Build the two acceptance gates that make the paraphrase probe interpretable: D-33 bucket
preservation and the D-34 bucket-aware lexical-divergence measurement, both computed in the agent's
own normalized token space and both proven to fail on violating input.

Purpose: F-05 makes constraint wording control which question unlocks a constraint. A paraphrase
that flips its `classify_constraint` bucket changes the *disclosure mechanics* as well as the
vocabulary, and the measured delta then mixes two effects and is uninterpretable. D-33 closes that.
D-34 then quantifies what the probe actually achieved, per bucket, so the reported divergence is a
measurement rather than an assertion — and the control arm's measured 0.9857 mean overlap becomes
the contrast that carries the Phase 7 headline.

D-51 corrects the version of `classify_constraint` these gates must be built on: it uses SEVEN
colour substrings at `local_evaluator.py:143`, not the twelve of `COLOR_RE` at `:24`, and it matches
by substring containment rather than `\b`. A gate built on the twelve-colour list pins the wrong
token and misreports overlap in both directions (L-4).

Output: `arena/datasets/divergence.py` and its two-sided test module.
</objective>
