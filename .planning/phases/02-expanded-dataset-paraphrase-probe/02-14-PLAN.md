<objective>
Prove the two properties plan 02-09's generator claims and cannot prove about itself: that the
control arm really is the public path (D-31, scoped by D-55), and that no probe-pipeline module can
reach retrieval (D-35, L-3).

Purpose: D-31's most valuable output is not the control arm, it is the free verification asset that
falls out of it — for the same target, a control-arm row (authored branch) and a bare row (fallback
branch) must drive byte-identical customer behavior. That test is what turns "our control reproduces
the public-set phrasing" from a claim into evidence, and Phase 7's entire framing of the probe delta
as "public-set phrasing vs customer phrasing" rests on it.

Two traps live here. L-2: the identity holds for `buying`, `browsing` and `boundary`, where
`behavior_for` returns `{"scenario_type": s}` and never touches the rng, but CANNOT hold for
`intent_override`, where D-36 pins `override["turn"]` from `pair_id` while the fallback draws
`rng.choice([3, 4])` from `f"{sample_id}\0{scenario_type}"`. The unscoped test is ~15% flaky and
reads as a corpus bug (D-55). L-3: `.planning/research/ARCHITECTURE.md:258` explicitly recommends a
solvability check, so a diligent implementer will add one to the probe pipeline — which would delete
precisely the sessions carrying the vocabulary gap, report ~0, and leave every gate green.

Split from plan 02-09 on scope grounds: 02-09 already carries a 330-line module, a five-gate
authoring loop and a 17-flag CLI across three tasks, and a four-class test module was a fourth. This
plan touches exactly one file, which no other wave-5 plan writes.

Output: `tests/test_datasets_control_fidelity.py`.
</objective>
