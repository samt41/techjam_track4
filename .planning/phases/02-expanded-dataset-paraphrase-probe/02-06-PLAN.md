<objective>
Build `arena/paired_contrast.py`: the one statistical readout the Phase 1 arena cannot currently
produce — ONE candidate measured once, partitioned into two arms by the `arm` field D-46 puts inside
each sample row, and joined on `pair_id` (D-44) — and exercise `adjudicate`'s D-45 cross-corpus
refusal, whose path only becomes reachable now that four distinct corpus digests exist (D-58: four,
not five — D-45's and D-48's "five" predates D-46's consolidation of the probe's three arms into a
single file).

Note on a reconciled discrepancy the executor will meet: `02-RESEARCH.md:754` and the
`02-VALIDATION.md` "D-45 inverse" row both assume control and probe live in two separate corpora and
therefore carry two different `dataset_sha256` values. D-46 and D-25 lock the opposite design — one
`probe.v1` corpus of 700 rows carrying `control`, `probe_sonnet` and `probe_haiku` arms — so both
arms come from one `run_arena run` and one digest. The guard is therefore built on arm labels and
disjoint `sample_id` sets, not on differing digests. That reconciliation is commented at the guard
and asserted in its test.

But permitting a shared digest must not become permitting a DIFFERENT one. Two arms drawn from two
corpora, joined on `pair_id`, is exactly the silently-bogus contrast D-45 exists to prevent, and it
is reachable through plan 02-10's `--probe-record` / `--probe-corpus` flags. This plan closes it
twice over: a differing `dataset_sha256` is refused unless the caller passes an explicit
`allow_cross_corpus=True` that the CLI never sets, and plan 02-03 namespaces `pair_id` with its
corpus stem so two corpora share no pair id and `align_on_pair_id` would raise on every one of them
anyway. The structural half is the one that cannot be forgotten; the flag exists so the refusal
reads as deliberate at the guard.

Purpose: control-vs-probe is a different statistical object from candidate-vs-candidate. Nothing
was selected from a pool of k, so `adjudicate`'s Holm family and winner's-curse correction are
meaningless here and must be visibly, deliberately absent rather than quietly skipped. The pairing
key also differs: `_require_paired` (`arena/statistics.py:107-117`) joins on `sample_id`, and
control and probe rows necessarily carry different `sample_id`s, so the guard rejects the arms
as-handed (L-8). The fix is a re-key at the call site with `dataclasses.replace`, never a weakening
of the guard — it is MEAS-04's structural guarantee.

Sequenced after plan 02-01 so every new `arena/` module lands under the widened boundary guard from
its first commit.

Output: `arena/paired_contrast.py` with its JSON record and Markdown view, its test module, and the
new D-45 refusal case in `tests/test_arena_adjudication.py`.
</objective>
